# OpenAI Decisions Kimi RAG aggregates

[`summary.json`](summary.json) holds the source-free numbers behind the [Decisions re-run section](../../README.md#openai-decisions-re-run-2026-10-06). It was generated by `publication/decisions-aggregates.py` in the private repository and lists every private source file it read with its SHA-256 (`sources`). It contains numbers and machine identifiers only; no EnronQA questions, reference answers, chunks, generated answers, receipts or raw provider responses.

Fields under `data`:

- `same_judge_scores` and `same_judge_paired`: answers accepted by `deepseek-v4p1-flash` for all three arms (unfiltered, frozen Jev filter, Decisions filter), and paired differences in points with 95% cluster-bootstrap intervals by source document (5,000 resamples, descriptive).
- `frozen_judge_reference` and `frozen_vs_rejudged_agreement`: the frozen `deepseek-v4-flash-0731` verdicts on the two frozen arms, and agreement between the two judge versions on the same answers.
- `jev_judge_scores`: the Jev answer judge's acceptances for all three arms.
- `filtering`: Decisions filter calls, pages, chunks scored and kept, and initial-search source coverage before and after filtering.
- `filter_vs_frozen_jev_on_identical_inputs`: chunk-level agreement with the frozen Jev filter where inputs were identical (counts, keep agreement, Cohen's kappa, mean probability difference).
- `refusals`, `latency` and `cost_estimates`: refusal counts (none), filter-page and Kimi answer timings, and list-price pipeline costs under three caching assumptions, including the Decisions price at which its pipeline would cost the same as Jev's. `decisions_arm_model_costs` breaks the Decisions arm down by model and phase.

Costs are list-price estimates (Decisions at $0.10 per million input tokens), not invoices; they cover answering, filtering, embedding and reranking, not judging or indexing.
